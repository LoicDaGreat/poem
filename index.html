A portrait in verse

Black Woman

A celebration of strength and grace

Dipped in chocolate, bronzed in elegance,
enamelled with grace, toasted with beauty,
my Lord she's a black woman.
Her strength comes in different shades and colours.

I could compare her to a sunflower.
On her darkest days she stands tall and faces the sun.
She owes nobody nothing, she is born worthy.

She's been through the furnace,
beware when she gazes into the fire and smiles.

Every shade carries its own light
