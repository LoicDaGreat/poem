A moment in verse

Where the Light
Finds Us

A little poem for the in-between

✳

The evening lays its amber hands
upon the roofs, the restless land;
the day unthreads its golden seam
and slips away into a dream.

Still, in the hush, a window glows—
a small, warm yes the dark bestows.
And all at once, the world feels kind:
there is a light that waits to find us.
